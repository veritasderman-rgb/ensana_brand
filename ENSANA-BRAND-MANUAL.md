## 2. Logo and name usage

### 2.1 Name architecture (Naming Systematic)

Ensana operates on **three brand levels** — always observe which level a piece of communication belongs to before choosing the name/lockup.

| Level | Written as | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand level** | `Ensana` + `Health Spa Hotels` (plural) | Corporate, group-wide, homepage of ensanahotels.com, generic brand ads, business cards of HQ staff |
| **Cluster level** | `[Cluster location]` + `Ensana Health Spa Hotels` (plural) | Regional communications. Clusters: Mariánské Lázně, Piešťany, Hungary, Smrdáky, Sovata, United Kingdom |
| **Hotel level** | `[Hotel name]` + `Ensana Health Spa Hotel` (singular!) | Single-property communications (stationery, signage, hotel-specific ads) |

> Rule: **Plural** ("Hotels") for brand and cluster level. **Singular** ("Hotel") at hotel level.

### 2.2 Property list (to be used verbatim)

- **Hungary cluster** — Thermal Margaret Island, Grand Margaret Island, Thermal Hévíz, Thermal Aqua, Thermal Sárvár
- **Mariánské Lázně cluster (CZ)** — Nové Lázně, Centrální Lázně, Hvězda, Pacifik, Butterfly, Vltava, Svoboda
- **Piešťany cluster (SK)** — Thermia Palace, Esplanade, Splendid, Pro Patria, Jalta, Vila Trajan
- **Smrdáky cluster (SK)** — Central, Vietoris, Morava
- **Sovata cluster (RO)** — Sovata, Bradet, Ursina
- **United Kingdom cluster** — Buxton Crescent

### 2.3 Logo mechanics

- **Primary lockup** consists of the word mark `ensana` paired with the descriptor `Health Spa Hotels` (or `Health Spa Hotel` at hotel level).
- **Cover size reference:** on A4-ish brochure covers the logo is printed at **30 mm** width.
- **Alignment on covers:** the logo is always **centre-aligned**.
- **Signature creative move (covers):** one letter of the main title is intentionally **intersected with the photography beneath** (ideally the middle letter of the word). Use sparingly and only on brand-level covers, not on information-heavy pages.
- **Clear space & minimum size:** keep generous clear space around the mark; never let other graphics or photo content touch the wordmark except for the deliberate cover-letter intersection described above.
- **Colour variants:**
  - Default on white: Indigo `#004F71`.
  - On dark photography or coloured backgrounds: **White** (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Monochrome black is allowed only when colour printing is impossible.

### 2.4 Naming applications matrix (summary)

| Channel | Brand level | Cluster level | Hotel level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website | Ensana Health Spa Hotels | `[Cluster]` + Ensana Health Spa Hotels | `[Hotel]` + Ensana Health Spa Hotel |
| Print ad signature | Ensana Health Spa Hotels | `[Cluster]` + Ensana Health Spa Hotels | `[Hotel]` + Ensana Health Spa Hotel |
| Business card | `Ensana / Health Spa Hotels` | `[Cluster] / Ensana Health Spa Hotels` | `[Hotel] / Ensana Health Spa Hotel` |
| Social media | `Ensana Health Spa Hotels@[Country or cz]` | same | same |
| Building signage | n/a | n/a | Hotel name + "Health Spa Hotel" below |

Always close online pieces with the call **"Find out more, visit, follow: ensanahotels.com"**.

> **Web addresses: always without "www."** — write `ensanahotels.com`, `marienbad.com`, never `www.ensanahotels.com`. Applies to print, digital, signatures, QR captions and copy in every language.

### 2.5 Don'ts

- Do not alter the wordmark spacing, kerning or letterforms.
- Do not add drop shadows, outlines, gradients or effects to the logo.
- Do not use the logo on busy, low-contrast backgrounds.
- Do not mix "Hotels" and "Hotel" — singular is reserved strictly for hotel-level lockups.
- Do not translate the descriptor.

## 3. Colour system

### 3.1 Primary palette

Primary colours form the base of every brand element. They must **not be changed or extended**. The primary range intentionally spans **cold (indigo) to warm (turquoise) tones**.

| Colour | Pantone (U) | Pantone (C) | CMYK | RGB | HEX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Indigo** | 308 U | 3025 C | 99 / 10 / 14 / 39 *(U)* · 100 / 27 / 10 / 56 *(C)* | 38 / 103 / 130 *(U)* · 0 / 79 / 113 *(C)* | `#266782` *(U)* · `#004F71` *(C)* |
| **Turquoise** | 636 U | 636 C | 36 / 0 / 9 / 0 *(U)* · 39 / 0 / 7 / 0 *(C)* | 138 / 216 / 237 *(U)* · 139 / 211 / 230 *(C)* | `#8AD8ED` *(U)* · `#8BD3E6` *(C)* |
| **White** | — | — | 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 | 255 / 255 / 255 | `#FFFFFF` |
| **Black** | — | — | 100 / 100 / 100 / 100 | 0 / 0 / 0 | `#000000` |

**Tint steps.** Indigo and Turquoise may be used at **80 %, 60 %, 40 % and 20 %** opacity/tint. Use tints for backgrounds, data-viz, soft section breaks.

Default digital/web usage:
- Indigo `#004F71`
- Turquoise `#8BD3E6`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Black `#000000`

### 3.2 Secondary palette

Used to expand the visual range. **Warm bordeaux + warm yellow + light dove grey.** Never combine more than two secondary colours on one page; primary palette always dominates.

| Secondary colour | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm Yellow** | Conference / MICE section (accent) | Used for title boxes and backgrounds in conference communications |
| **Warm Grey** | Wedding section + back covers of MICE brochures | Neutral, supportive colour |
| **Warm Bordeaux** | Accent for editorial / data-viz | Use sparingly for contrast |

> Exact secondary Pantone/CMYK/RGB/HEX values live in the Ensana Frontify style guide. When values are not available, match visually to "warm yellow ≈ `#E4C158`", "warm grey ≈ `#B3ADA3`", "warm bordeaux ≈ `#7A2A3A`" and flag the approximation in the file.

### 3.3 Section colour coding (MICE + Spa toolkit)

When designing for the multi-section brochure system:

| Section | Background colour | Title box colour |
|---|---|---|
| Spa / Medical | Indigo | Turquoise text, Indigo box |
| Conference / MICE | Warm Yellow | White text on Warm Yellow box |
| Wedding | Warm Grey | White text on Warm Grey box |

### 3.4 Do / Don't

- **Do** keep primaries dominant; secondaries are accents.
- **Do** use Turquoise for emphasis and headline "pop".
- **Do** use Indigo for body text over white.
- **Don't** introduce colours outside the defined palette.
- **Don't** oversaturate photography to match the palette — keep images naturally warm.

- Keep body text ≥ 11 pt in print and ≥ 14 px on web for legibility.
- Use Bold/Semibold for hierarchy, never all-caps for body.

## 5. Imagery

### 5.1 Tonality (the "filter" every image must pass)

> **warm · authentic · confident**

Every image should feel like a spontaneous, credible insight into life at Ensana — never staged. Protagonists interact genuinely with Ensana products, services and places.

Three qualities every Ensana image has:
1. **Meaningful situations** — real, captured moments.
2. **Real emotions** — no forced smiles, no stock-photo posing.
3. **Relevant subject matter** — positive, credible, convincing depictions of brand/products.

### 5.2 The three image types

| Type | Use for | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| **Metaphoric** | Brand / image level, covers, brand films | Abstract natural structures, close-ups, wide angles, dominated by indigo + turquoise and related natural tones (green, grey, blue, brown) |
| **Lifestyle** | Product / service brochures, subpages, product ads | People in real settings interacting with services; one core action per shot; natural light, light beige whites; optional subtle Ensana colour accents |
| **Location** | Hotel pages, brochure inners, booking platforms | Interiors and surroundings; clean but not sterile; natural perspective; warm beige whites; no strong artificial light |

Plus **documentary** variants:
- **Event photography** — lively, spontaneous, no heavy flash.
- **Process photography** — expertise and know-how; natural colour.
- **Staff portraits** — in working environment, working clothes, friendly gaze into camera, soft but identifiable background.

### 5.3 Technical / formal criteria (all types)

- **Composition.** Clear, calm, reduced. Natural perspective — unusual only for metaphoric work.
- **Light.** Atmospheric ambient light; natural mood; avoid strong shadows and flash.
- **Colour.** Warm, natural, friendly. Large white areas should read as **warm light beige**, not cold white. Selective accents in Indigo / Turquoise allowed.
- **Focus.** Sharp/soft focus as didactic tool to guide the eye; never lose the main motif in blur.
- **Post-production.** For existing images, colour-grade toward warm tones, apply moderate blur for depth, crop to match the defined style. Never over-saturate.

### 5.4 Photo layout rules (brochures)

- Main article photos should **bleed to the edge of the page** (one or both page edges).
- When using more than one photo: align them to the **page edge on one side** and to the **paragraph edge on the other**; position evenly on the page.
- On main pages use **max. two photos per page**.
- Avoid dark photos; ensure tonal consistency across a spread.
- Titles set over dark photography → use **white** Branding Bold.

### 5.5 Don'ts (all image types)

- ✗ Stereotyped portrayal
- ✗ Obviously posed / staged images
- ✗ Unprofessional-looking shots
- ✗ Unnatural, artificial or contrived motifs
- ✗ Questionable messaging
- ✗ Inharmonious proportions
- ✗ Dark atmosphere or overexposed images
- ✗ Strong artificial light / hard flash
- ✗ Dominant non-brand colours, oversaturation
- ✗ Extreme perspectives (except metaphoric)
- ✗ Lack of focus / indistinct main motif
- ✗ Staff portraits showing hierarchy or cut-outs

## 6. Tone of voice & copy

### 6.1 Voice attributes

- **Warm** — hospitable, human, inviting.
- **Authentic** — no marketing fluff; credible, specific facts.
- **Confident** — Ensana is a leader; write with quiet authority, not superlatives.
- **Empowering** — give the reader agency over their own wellbeing ("Be your own health manager").

### 6.2 Recurring concepts & phrases

- "Let yourself flow"
- "Be your own health manager"
- "How to increase your power"
- "Aspects of vitality"
- "Feel better"
- Call-to-action closer: **"Discover the ensana health spa hotels at ensanahotels.com"**
- Social follow line: **"Find out more, visit, follow: ensanahotels.com"**

### 6.3 Writing rules

- Sentence case for titles; Title Case only in proper names (hotel names, cluster names).
- Address the reader directly ("your stay", "your treatment plan").
- Prefer concrete nouns (thermal mineral water, healing mud, Mirror Pool) over vague adjectives.
- Medical content: factual, neutral, non-alarmist; clearly list indications *and* contraindications.
- Avoid marketing clichés ("the ultimate", "world-class", "one-of-a-kind").
- Write web addresses without "www." (`ensanahotels.com`, `marienbad.com`).
- Use English spelling consistent with the hotel country (EN-GB default for European audiences).

## 7. Applications & templates

### 7.1 Business cards

Three layouts — one per brand level. Common structure:

```
[Level lockup]
————————————
First name Last name
Position
[Legal entity]
[Address]
Tel.: +XXX XXX XXX XXX – Mobile: +XXX XXX XXX XXX
Email: name.surname@ensanahotels.com
ensanahotels.com
```

Level-specific legal entities / addresses:
- **Brand (HQ)** — Arisende s.r.o., Palác Kronberg, 3rd Floor, Senovážné náměstí 992/8, CZ-110 00 Prague 1
- **Cluster — Piešťany** — Slovenské Liečebné Kúpele Piešťany, a.s., Winterova 29, SK-921 29 Piešťany
- **Cluster — Mariánské Lázně** — Léčebné lázně Mariánské Lázně a.s., Mariánské Lázně, Czech Republic

### 7.2 Email signature

```
First name Surname
Position
[Address line — preferably in one line]
Mobile: +XXX XXX XXX XXX
Email: name.surname@ensanahotels.com
ensanahotels.com
```

Followed by the confidentiality notice:

> This message and any attachment are confidential and may also be privileged or otherwise protected. If you are not the intended recipient, you may not copy, distribute or disclose its content to any person or entity, use it for any purpose or store or copy the information in any medium. If you have received it in error, please notify the sender immediately and delete the message from your systems. Personal messages and/or opinions in a message are solely those of the author. The company will accept no responsibility for such messages.

Three levels (HQ / Cluster / Hotel) differ only in the address line and which lockup appears above the name.

### 7.3 Stationery

Hotel-level letterheads carry:
- Hotel name + `Ensana Health Spa Hotel` descriptor
- Full postal address of the hotel
- Contact details (telephone, reservation, email, web)
- Registered legal entity, Reg. No., VAT No.

### 7.4 Print advertising

- **Cover signature** after the slogan: `[Hotel / Cluster / Brand lockup]`
- **Standalone logo** for print ads: always the word mark `Ensana` (no descriptor) when space is tight.
- **Closing line:** "Discover the ensana health spa hotels at ensanahotels.com"

### 7.5 Web / Google presence

- Page title in search: `ensana | Health Spa Hotels`
- URL: `ensanahotels.com`
- Meta description: short, benefit-led sentence in brand voice.

### 7.6 Building / on-property

- **Building canopy & door signage:** hotel name only (no brand lockup on physical surfaces).
- **Entrance door plate / brass sign:** `Ensana` + logo.
- **Welcome mat:** not branded.
- **Elevators:** no posters.
- **Walls / windows / other physical surfaces:** not branded.

### 7.7 Operational materials (in-room)

- Key cards, personal name tags, door hangers, menus, spa tent cards — carry the **Ensana Health Spa Hotels** wordmark.
- Memos and envelopes at hotel level follow the hotel-level lockup + logo + url + social handle structure.
- Thematic paper variants exist for: General, Health, Wellness, Sport/Fitness.

### 7.8 Brochure system

Two toolkits exist — use the correct one:

1. **Medical / Spa toolkit** — Indigo + Turquoise primary palette; Medical Information cover; type scale from §4.2; photos bleed to page edge; titles 60 pt Branding Bold in Turquoise (or white on dark photo).
2. **MICE toolkit** — split into three sections (Conference → Warm Yellow, Wedding → Warm Grey, Spa → Indigo); larger cover titles (up to 125 pt); back cover in Warm Grey.

### 7.9 Maps

- Use **only primary Ensana colours** for map design.
- When the map dominates the page, use a **white background**.
- Otherwise set the map on Turquoise or Indigo tint.
